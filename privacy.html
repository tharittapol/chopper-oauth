Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Chopper is a personal assistant app. Its only user is the owner of the GitHub account tharittapol, who also runs it.

Data accessed

Chopper accesses only its owner's Google account, and only the data he grants on Google's consent screen, such as files in Google Drive, Google Docs and Google Sheets.

How the data is used

Chopper uses the data only to carry out its owner's requests, for example to find or read a file he names. It sends, changes, deletes or shares data only after he approves that action.

Storage and sharing

The access token and any files Chopper fetches are stored on the owner's own computer. To answer a request, Chopper sends the relevant content to the AI model service that powers it and shows the answer in the owner's private chat. The data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with anyone else.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke Chopper's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
